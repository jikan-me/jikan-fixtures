I would like to share the core concept and deeper meaning of my novel, God of Calamity.
The story revolves around a prophecy that cannot be changed, not even by the gods themselves. It explores a profound question: What happens when a person tries to fight a fate that even the gods cannot escape?

At its heart, this is a story about how calamity is not always born from evil, but can emerge from the actions, greed, selfishness, and betrayal of those who create it themselves.
The protagonist begins his journey as a hero, but the cruelty of the world gradually transforms him. Betrayed by his friends, deceived by society, and consumed by the selfishness of those around him, he loses everything he holds dear, including his beloved and, eventually, his own sense of self. The pain of these losses destroys the person he once was.
Refusing to accept the fate imposed upon him, he decides to fight for his freedom, his lost love, and everything that has been taken from him. However, his desire to challenge destiny becomes a force of destruction. His anger turns against the world, and the hero who once lived within him begins to emerge as something far more terrifying: a calamity shaped by the very world that betrayed him.
Yet, above everything, the prophecy remains unbroken. Even when the gods interfere, they discover that some destinies cannot be rewritten.

The deeper meaning of the novel is that the line between a hero and a villain can be thinner than we imagine. Sometimes, the world creates the very monster it later fears. It also explores whether freedom is truly possible when destiny has already decided the outcome, and whether a person can fight against fate without becoming the very destruction they seek to overcome.
Ultimately, God of Calamity is not simply a story about a man who becomes a villain. It is a story about a broken soul challenging the authority of destiny, a world facing the consequences of its own cruelty, and a prophecy that stands beyond even the power of the gods.
My intention is to create a dark, emotionally powerful, and thought-provoking narrative that makes readers question the true nature of good and evil, the consequences of human choices, and whether fate is something we must accept or something worth sacrificing everything to challenge.
